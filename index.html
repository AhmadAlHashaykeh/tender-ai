FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does TenderAI generate price recommendations?

TenderAI uses advanced machine learning algorithms to analyze historical tender data, competitor pricing patterns, market trends, and win/loss ratios to generate intelligent bid recommendations with confidence scores.

Can I import my existing tender data?

Yes! TenderAI supports Excel and CSV imports with automatic data validation, cleaning, and standardization. Our AI engine processes your historical data to provide immediate insights.

What countries and markets does TenderAI support?

TenderAI supports pharmaceutical tender data from over 40 countries across the Middle East, Africa, and Asia. We continuously expand our coverage based on customer needs.

Is my tender data secure?

Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade encryption, secure cloud infrastructure, and strict access controls. Your data is never shared with competitors or third parties.

How accurate are the AI recommendations?

Our AI recommendations typically achieve 92%+ win optimization rates. Accuracy depends on the quality and volume of historical data available for analysis.
